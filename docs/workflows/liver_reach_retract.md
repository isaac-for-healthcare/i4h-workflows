# Liver reach and retract

`liver_reach_retract` composes Numerical Sim mechanics, the i4h–Isaac Lab host lifecycle and Arena scoring. Normal execution uses Arena policies; training stays in `rl/`.

## Physical contract

Full-cycle success requires reach, acquisition of the intended patch, 0.5 seconds of retention, 20 mm retraction, a one-second hold within 2 mm, release of both grasp mechanisms within 0.5 seconds, then one second of open relaxation. The deadline is 20 seconds and counts as failure. Reach-only and grasped-retract are separately labeled curricula.

Targets remain `252`, `257`, `255`, `1688`, `1098`. Physics runs at 60 Hz and control at 30 Hz. Four normalized action channels command Cartesian displacement and grip; displacement is limited to a 1 mm vector per control tick, applied once. Orientation stays fixed and energy/fire remain zero. The original mechanics, starts, ±5 mm jitter, 57-feature encoder and physical scaling are preserved.

The Arena reducer owns phases, timers, rewards and terminal results. Its post-physics callback observes both physics steps. Observation and reward getters do not advance state. Masked reset/restore preserves peer continuation, grasp identities and task history.

## Setup and offline snapshots

The current integration pin is numerical source `7edecea` plus the tracked bridge patch. That source and the consolidated bridge are included in internal Numerical Sim PR #19. **Public release still requires syncing the numerical source to the public repository and repinning setup, lockfiles and source paths to its public SHA.**

Run `./setup.sh` after the numerical revision is publicly fetchable. Supply its production assets as described by Numerical Sim. Generate compatible settled bundles offline before normal execution:

```bash
export I4H_LIVER_SNAPSHOTS="$PWD/runs/liver_reach_retract/snapshots"
PYTHONPATH="$PWD/rl" arena/.venv/bin/python scripts/prepare_liver_snapshots.py \
  --worlds 1 --output "$I4H_LIVER_SNAPSHOTS" --headless
```

Generate separate bundles for 8 and 16 environments when needed. Grasped-retract requires additional offline qualified grasped bundles generated by `i4h_rl.liver_experiment prepare-grasped`. Changed models, sources, asset paths or world counts require regenerated bundles. Incompatible identities and layouts are rejected; do not edit their hashes.

## Normal execution

```bash
./run.sh liver_reach_retract --idle
./run.sh liver_reach_retract --rule-based --episodes 1 --record
./run.sh liver_reach_retract --policy --checkpoint /path/to/exported/policy.pt --episodes 1
./run.sh liver_reach_retract --replay /path/to/demos.hdf5
```

The YAML declaration selects `LiverReferencePolicy`, `LiverExportedPolicy` or the open-jaw idle policy. The normal policy runner and training registration both construct the same custom numerical host. A small Arena dependency patch preserves that entry point in stock Arena tools.

Isaac Lab recorder terms store actions, observations and complete numerical/probe/task/RNG continuation under `initial_state/i4h`. The host restores this state after normal host reset/kinematic synchronization. Replay requires the recorded world count and compatible model identity. Legacy i4h-runtime HDF5 files use a different format; preserve them as experiment evidence.

The pinned Isaac Lab `replay_demos.py` currently fails headless while constructing its keyboard (`omni.appwindow` unavailable). Use visible native replay. This upstream tool limitation does not change the numerical continuation contract.

## Learning and evidence

The [RL profile](../../rl/profiles/liver_reach_retract.yaml) and [trainer configuration](../../rl/config/liver_reach_retract_ppo_rsl_rl.yaml) retain two 128-unit ELU layers, 16 environments, 256 control ticks per rollout, actor LR `1e-5`, desired KL `.01`, initial fixed std `.02` and disabled running observation normalization.

```bash
./train.sh rl show liver_reach_retract
./train.sh rl liver_reach_retract --dry-run
```

Training requires hashed BC inputs and a qualification manifest through `I4H_LIVER_BC` and `I4H_LIVER_QUALIFICATION`. The manifest covers isolation, replay, scoring, BC/distribution parity, zero-learning-rate invariance and critic-only invariance. Source changes require requalification. Ten critic-only updates precede actor updates. Mean update KL above `.02` rolls back actor/distribution and optimizer state. Same-start evaluation at actor updates 1, 5 and 10 stops training after a decrease exceeding two successes.

Native export writes `policy.pt` and `contract.json`. Normal workflow execution rejects curriculum exports. Native resume restores the environment, actor, critic, optimizer, safeguard counters and RNG; pending-validation checkpoints cannot resume and stopped checkpoints remain stopped.

The earlier runtime campaign achieved controller **100/100**, BC **94/100** and stopped PPO **59/100** on identical reserved starts. Full-cycle PPO stopped at actor update 5 after validation fell from 20/20 to 12/20. Both curricula retained 20/20 at updates 1, 5 and 10. These are historical results, not a repeated training campaign on the consolidated native runtime. Single-episode native smoke successes do not establish qualified full-cycle PPO.

Raw recordings, checkpoints, snapshots and the frozen earlier source/evidence remain local. They are excluded from Git; public artifact distribution and a fresh-clone qualification remain release work.
